What CleanScore is — and isn't

CleanScore is an independent, quarterly compliance audit. Every visit covers three things: ATP bioluminescence testing of your ice machine, reach-in coolers, soda lines, and bar guns; infrared temperature verification across the same equipment; and a visual inspection of gaskets, seals, and surfaces for wear or contamination. You receive a documented score against the FDA standard of under 10 RLU, every visit, on file.

This is a measurement service, not a cleaning service. If your equipment needs cleaning, CleanScore will tell you so, clearly, with a number — but the cleaning itself is a separate conversation, whether that's your existing provider or our full-service plan.

Not included: cleaning, sanitization, repairs, or mechanical service. CleanScore measures and documents — it doesn't replace whoever currently maintains your equipment.
