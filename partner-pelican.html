The Repair vs. Clean Distinction

A Repaired Hoshizaki Is Not Necessarily a Clean One

Your Pelican Aire technician restored your machine to full mechanical operation — the refrigerant cycle, harvest mechanism, water distribution system. That's exactly what they're trained and authorized to do. What warranty service cannot certify is the biological cleanliness of the ice your customers are putting in their drinks.

Mold, biofilm, bacteria, and mineral scale accumulate in water lines, ice bins, and evaporator plates whether or not the machine runs correctly. Florida's humidity and high-volume use accelerate contamination. The only way to know your machine is clean is to measure it with ATP bioluminescence testing.

Hoshizaki Warranty & Cleaning Requirement

Hoshizaki America requires semi-annual professional cleaning and sanitization to maintain warranty coverage. Your Pelican Aire service record covers the mechanical repair. A Pinellas Ice Co. ATP service record covers the cleaning compliance. Together, your warranty documentation is complete.
