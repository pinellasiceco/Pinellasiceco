Crystal Ice "Compliance Docs"
A printed service record that says a technician visited on a specific date. It lists what was done: sanitization, scale removal, inspection. There is no number. There is no measurement. There is no proof the machine is actually clean after the visit. A health inspector looking at this document cannot verify the result — only that someone showed up.
Pinellas Ice Co. ATP Certificate
A Hygiena EnSURE V2 luminometer swab test records the exact RLU score before cleaning and after. FDA acceptable standard is 10 RLU. Most uncleaned machines test above 200. Your CleanScore certificate shows the pre-clean number, the post-clean number, technician ID, date, and a QR code any inspector can scan to verify in real time. That's not a visit record — that's proof.
The Inspection Reality

When a DBPR inspector cites your establishment for ice machine contamination, a service record showing "cleaning performed" does not resolve the citation. A documented ATP score showing post-clean RLU below 10 does. One is a paper trail. The other is evidence. Crystal Ice gives you the first. We give you the second.
