The Repair vs. Clean Distinction

A Repaired Machine Is Not Necessarily a Clean Machine

When your Coastline Cooling technician services your ice machine, they restore mechanical function — refrigerant cycle, water distribution, compressor performance. What they cannot certify is the biological cleanliness of the ice your customers consume.

Mold, biofilm, bacteria, and mineral scale accumulate in water lines, ice bins, and evaporator plates regardless of whether the machine is running correctly. Florida's humidity and high-volume operation accelerate contamination. The only way to know your machine is truly clean is to measure it.
