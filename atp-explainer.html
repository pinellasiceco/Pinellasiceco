What ATP Testing Actually Is

The Science
ATP stands for Adenosine Triphosphate — the energy molecule present in all living cells. Every biological contaminant in a machine leaves ATP behind: bacteria, mold, biofilm, yeast, food residue. An ATP swab test measures the total biological load on a surface by detecting light emitted when ATP reacts with a testing reagent. The result — an RLU score — is a direct, objective measurement of contamination. Higher number means more contamination.
The Instrument
We use the Hygiena EnSURE V2 luminometer — the same technology used in hospital sanitation audits, food manufacturing quality control, and pharmaceutical clean room verification. It is not a consumer product. The result is documented digitally with a timestamp, location, technician ID, and RLU score.

The Objection We Hear
"Our machine looks clean — we wipe it down regularly."

A machine can look completely clean and test at 280 RLU. Biofilm — the primary contaminant in commercial ice machines — forms in water-contact surfaces that are never visible during normal operation. Scale buildup traps biological material in layers that visual inspection cannot detect. The EnSURE V2 finds what eyes miss.
