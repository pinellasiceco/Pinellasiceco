What a new machine's warranty covers
3 YEARS

Full machine — all parts and labor

Control boards ($300–600), pumps ($200–400), sensors, fans — everything covered in full for 3 years. This is the window when install issues and early component failures are most likely. Confirmed on both Hoshizaki and Manitowoc Indigo NXT.

5 YEARS

Evaporator — parts and labor

The evaporator is the most contamination-prone component and one of the costliest to replace ($400–800). Both parts and labor are fully covered for 5 years — the strongest tier of coverage on either brand.

5 YR PARTS
3 YR LABOR

Compressor — parts only after year 3

Compressor parts are covered for 5 years, but labor coverage ends at year 3. A compressor failure in year 4 or 5 means the part is free — but you pay $300–500 in labor. Budget for this in years 4–5 of ownership.

NOT COVERED

Scale buildup, cleaning failures, and filters

Manufacturer warranties explicitly exclude damage from scale, hard water, and inadequate maintenance. Probe failures and sensor errors caused by mineral buildup are not warranty claims — they are maintenance failures. Water filter replacements (~$75–150/year) are also owner responsibility.

IMPORTANT

Cleaning documentation protects your warranty

Both Hoshizaki and Manitowoc require proof of proper professional maintenance to honor warranty claims. A machine that develops scale or mold due to neglect can have claims denied. Our quarterly CleanScore report is that documentation — dated, signed, and on file after every visit — so your warranty stays valid and your claims go through.
