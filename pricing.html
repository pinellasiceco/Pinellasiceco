Common Questions

How often do you come out?
Every 90 days — four times per year. We schedule the visits in advance and send a reminder before each one. FDA and NSF guidelines recommend cleaning commercial ice machines at minimum every 6 months. We do it quarterly because more frequent cleaning means lower contamination levels and a faster clean each visit.
How long does a visit take?
Typically 60–90 minutes depending on machine size and contamination level. We work around your schedule — we can come before open, during slow hours, or at close.
Do I have to sign a contract?
No contract. Month-to-month billing. If you want to cancel, give us 30 days notice. We'll process your last visit and close the account. No fees, no questions.
What if my machine fails the ATP test after cleaning?
We don't leave until it passes. If the post-clean RLU score is above 10, we clean again. The certificate is only issued when the machine meets standard.
Do you handle repairs?
Cleaning and sanitation only — that's our specialty. If we spot a mechanical issue during a visit we'll tell you what we observed and recommend a refrigeration service provider. We don't upsell repairs we didn't come to do.
Can I start with just one visit?
Yes. The free inspection is always no-obligation. After that, most customers start with a one-time initial service before enrolling in the quarterly plan — especially if there's a DBPR citation to address first.
