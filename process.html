The Six Steps

1
Arrival & DBPR Review
5 minutes
Before touching the machine, we pull your establishment's current DBPR inspection record. We review any active citations, prior ice machine violations, and scheduled inspection windows. You get a summary of where you stand before we start.
2
Pre-Clean ATP Test
5 minutes
We swab the ice-contact surfaces using a Hygiena EnSURE V2 luminometer before any cleaning begins. This establishes your baseline RLU score — the number that shows exactly how contaminated the machine is right now.

3
Disassembly & Inspection
10 minutes
We remove accessible panels and components to reach all water-contact surfaces. We inspect for visible mold, biofilm, scale buildup, filter condition, and any mechanical issues worth noting.
  • ● Ice-making components and evaporator plates
  • ● Water distribution system and inlet lines
  • ● Ice bin interior and bin liner
  • ● Air filters and condenser (if accessible)
4
Descale & Deep Clean
30 – 45 minutes
The core of the service. We use a Nucalgon descaler to break down mineral deposits and scale buildup, followed by a full sanitization of all water-contact surfaces. Removes biofilm, mold, bacteria, and the scale that traps contamination between visits.
  • ● Descaling cycle — mineral and scale removal
  • ● Sanitization of evaporator, distribution lines, bin
  • ● Manual scrubbing of accessible surfaces
  • ● Flush and rinse cycle before reassembly
5
Post-Clean ATP Verification
5 minutes
We swab the same surfaces again and run a second ATP test. The machine must score at or below 10 RLU to pass. If it doesn't, we clean again. We don't leave until it passes — the certificate is only issued when the standard is met.
6
Certification & Sticker
5 minutes
We issue a CleanScore certificate showing both RLU scores, the date, technician ID, and your establishment. A QR cert sticker goes on the machine — any health inspector, manager, or customer can scan it to verify the result in real time.
